# Employee Salary Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze employee salaries, workforce characteristics, and factors associated with annual salary.

This project uses an **Employee Compensation & Salary Prediction dataset** containing 1,000 employee records and explores salary patterns across experience, skills, performance, education, job roles, city tiers, and work type.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview
The dashboard contains three analytical pages:

### 1. Salary Overview
Provides a high-level view of employee salary patterns.
**Key visuals:**
* Total Employees
* Average Salary
* Median Salary
* Maximum Salary
* Average Experience
* Salary Distribution
* Average Salary by Education Level
* Average Salary by Job Role
* Average Salary by City Tier
* Interactive salary and employee filters
  
### 2. Salary Analysis
Explores the relationship between employee characteristics and salary.
**Key visuals:**
* Average Salary by Years of Experience
* Average Salary by Number of Skills
* Average Salary by Performance Score
* Salary Summary by Job Role
* Key Findings
  
The summary table compares:
* Average Salary
* Average Experience
* Average Skills
* Average Performance Score
* Employee Count

### 3. Employee Profile
Analyzes the composition of the workforce.
**Key visuals:**
* Employees by Job Role
* Employees by Education Level
* Employees by Years of Experience
* Employees by City Tier
* Employees by Work Type
* Key Findings
---
## 💡 Key Insights
Some of the main observations from the analysis include:
* Average salary increases strongly with years of experience.
* Skill count shows a positive but less consistent relationship with salary.
* Salary differences across performance scores are relatively modest.
* Tier 1 employees have a higher average salary than Tier 2 and Tier 3 employees.
* Higher education levels show a gradual increase in average salary.
* Employee distribution across remote and on-site work is relatively balanced.
* Experience shows the strongest visible relationship with salary among the factors analyzed.
